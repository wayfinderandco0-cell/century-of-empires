ABOUT THIS LANDSCAPE

Geography beneath the art.

This isolated visual pilot shows Navis valley in the northern Brenner region. The pass summit itself lies farther south.

Mountains
The existing V97 Copernicus elevation and relief. The retained numeric grid is roughly 160–230 m between samples. Fine rock textures are illustrative, not new survey detail.
Water
Existing V99 OpenStreetMap natural streams. Modern mapped courses are geographic guides, not a verified 1800 survey. Artificial ditches are omitted.
Woodland & trail
Reconstructed vegetation using an ESA woodland proxy, slope and elevation. The unpaved valley trail is illustrative; its historical alignment is not authenticated.
Four scales
The camera moves continuously through the same coordinates. Atlas and regional views cover the retained Alpine data only. A full continental transition and military integration remain future work.

Elevation: Copernicus GLO-30 via V97 · Woodland proxy: ESA WorldCover 2021 · Water © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. Original V105 canopy artwork with V97-derived measured geography; no aerial photographs or modern transport layers.
