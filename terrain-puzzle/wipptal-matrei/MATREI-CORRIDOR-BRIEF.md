# Matrei — first historically supported Brenner travel-corridor cell

Status: **Geographic study, NOT road/art approval, no live movement**.

The 1780–1880 century covers the established Brenner crossing, including 1777 carriage-road upgrades. Matrei was a historic market settlement arranged along the major trade road. The original Navis/Fidlerbach Octagon 001 has no approved through-trails; **do not force the game to use that side valley**.

**This Matrei project does not manufacture an 1800 road polyline.** It obtains present-day OpenStreetMap Brennerstraße/B182 street geometry through the village and clips it to a 1km octagon centred at latitude 47.130278, longitude 11.453056. This centre is geolocated on the historical Brennerstraße, using a Wikimedia Commons historic streetscape photograph coordinate, but the street's modern geometry is only a GUIDE and should be checked against actual historical maps before approving precise campaign routing.

The builder creates developer-only controls using the previously retained V97 geographic relief and elevation grid. No asphalt, modern bridge, railway or motorway is introduced into game artwork. The street control is not the final 1780s paving or road width.
