PROTOTYPE 11 — Designed for phone browsers. Countries and relationships are historically inspired, but city locations, province connections, resource concentrations, strength bonuses, and battles are a simplified game model—not historical resource surveys. No purchases or payment processing are enabled. Save is local to this browser only, and this version is not online multiplayer. All named game brands are independent of this prototype.
1780 · A continent on the brink

Who will shape the century?

Take command of a major nation. Other kingdoms, republics, and empires will develop, wage wars, and form alliances on their own. Small AI countries can rise into great powers. Grain regions feed armies, ore-rich hills forge weapons, and trading ports bring wealth.

Mobile demo · schematic map · one game month every five real minutes · no pause · single-player AI prototype.
