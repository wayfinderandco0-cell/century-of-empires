## Why this experiment differs from V238

V238's broad brown road strip visibly looked pasted over a meadow in the actual screenshot. V241 retains the original grass median and shoulders, changes only two narrow wheel ruts, and uses source-photo high-frequency grain and illumination. It reads and pins the original V123 graph blob; it does not move route points, add settlements, copy landmarks, invent a bridge, or pretend the photograph contains a historical road. The output is diagnostic only. The actual original WEST photo is the Stijn te Strake CC0 1.0 Austrian meadow already SHA256-pinned in V237/V238. Its source page is https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Forested_mountain_valley_(Unsplash).jpg. This is modern source material and not an independently registered Matrei/V123 or 1780 road source.
