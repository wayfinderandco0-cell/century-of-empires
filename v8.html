PROTOTYPE 07 — Designed for phone browsers. Countries and relationships are historically inspired, but political overlays, 1780 borders, province connections, resources, strengths, and battle models are illustrative rather than verified historical boundaries or surveys. Physical coastline uses GSHHS relief coastline data. No purchases or payment processing are enabled. Save is local to this browser only, and this version is not online multiplayer. All named game brands are independent of this prototype.
1780 · A continent on the brink

Who will shape the century?

Take command of a major nation. Other kingdoms, republics, and empires will develop, wage wars, and form alliances on their own. Small AI countries can rise into great powers. Grain regions feed armies, ore-rich hills forge weapons, and trading ports bring wealth. Real coastlines, forests, rivers, mountains, and sea crossings shape your campaigns.

Mobile demo · geographic atlas map · free play · no Goldmarks or paid military advantages.
