Century of Empires

Goal: control all 12 Alpine provinces. WEST, MATREI and SOUTH begin under your banner; the eastern frontier belongs to a rival empire. Only Matrei is a major historical town in this scenario.

Play: tap an octagon, then March / Conquer. Your army follows connected roads through your provinces and may attack one neighboring rival or neutral province per order. You have two orders per turn. The 12-cell artwork is an unapproved prototype; route lines are schematic gameplay links, not surveyed historical roads.

Economy: capturing territory earns income each turn. Recruiting costs 60 gold for 90 soldiers (only where your army stands). Fortify an owned province for 45 gold to add 50 defenders. Each march costs 8 supplies, plus 4 supplies per turn.

Combat: mountain and fortress terrain strengthen defenders. A battle resolves when the march arrives. Your army loses soldiers; strong defenders may repel an attack. The rival can expand or attack when you end a turn. Progress is saved on this device when storage is permitted.

This is the first offline playable 12-province campaign, not the complete historical strategy game. Province movement, conquest, recruits, turns, AI and offline save are implemented. Roads, crossings, terrain approval and the extended historical world remain prototype work.
