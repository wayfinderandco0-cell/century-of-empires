The closest view uses official Austrian orthophotography from basemap.at (CC BY 4.0) for recognizable real tree crowns, with real local streams from OpenStreetMap (ODbL). These are modern observations, not reconstructed 1780 forests, and no military-pathfinding integration has been done. V94 and campaign saves remain unchanged.
